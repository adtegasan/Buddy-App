# Pet System Design

## Purpose

The pet is not a game.

The pet is a visualization of workplace health.

---

## Attributes

### Hunger

Represents neglected responsibilities.

Affected by:

- unread messages
- unanswered emails
- overdue actions
- pending requests

---

### Energy

Represents workload strain.

Affected by:

- meeting overload
- lack of focus time
- excessive work volume

---

### Mood

Represents momentum.

Affected by:

- task completion
- progress
- achievements

---

### Confidence

Represents workplace control.

Affected by:

- overdue items
- missing submissions
- unresolved commitments

---

### Experience

Represents long-term progress.

Awarded when:

- tasks complete
- requests finish
- priorities handled

---

### Level

Determined by experience.

Used for future growth systems.
