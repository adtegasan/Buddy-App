# Enterprise Buddy

## Overview

Enterprise Buddy is a desktop AI companion for enterprise and corporate workers.

The assistant is represented as a virtual pet named Byte.

Unlike traditional productivity applications, Byte's wellbeing reflects the user's attention to important workplace responsibilities.

The goal is to create emotional engagement around completing important work.

The application combines:

- Productivity coaching
- AI assistance
- Desktop companionship
- Workplace awareness
- Pet simulation

The pet serves as a visual representation of workplace health.

---

## The Problem

Corporate workers are constantly overwhelmed by:

- Teams messages
- Emails
- Action items
- Meeting follow-ups
- Manager requests
- Deadlines
- Time sheet submissions

Traditional systems use notifications and dashboards.

Users eventually ignore them.

---

## The Solution

Instead of another dashboard, Enterprise Buddy creates an emotional connection.

Users care for Byte.

Byte's health reflects their workplace habits.

When work is ignored:

- Byte becomes hungry
- Byte becomes sad
- Byte becomes worried
- Byte becomes tired

When work is completed:

- Byte becomes happier
- Byte gains energy
- Byte becomes healthier
- Byte levels up

The user is motivated by caring for Byte, not by earning points.

---

## Example

Unread Teams Messages = 6

↓

Byte becomes hungry.

Overdue Tasks = 4

↓

Byte becomes worried.

Manager Request Due Today

↓

Byte becomes anxious.

User completes actions

↓

Byte improves visually and emotionally.
